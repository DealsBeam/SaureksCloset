# Bags

Each look can carry up to five bags, including multiple copies of a model.
Choose from eight bag styles. **Cloth Pouch** includes four colors: Burgundy,
Navy, Ochre and Olive.

1. Open **Bags**. All five slots are always visible. Click an empty slot to open its tuner and visual model picker. The slot stays empty until you choose a bag; closing the picker does not add anything.
2. Choose a style by its bag icon, then choose its **Start** position in the tuner: Back, Left hip or Right hip. For Cloth Pouch, use the **Color** swatches. Click **Model** to change styles later. Escape closes the model picker while keeping the tuner open.
3. Adjust position, rotation and size. **Save Fit** stores those values with this bag in the current look.
4. All five slots fit on one page. Click a filled slot or its bag icon to tune it; the active slot is highlighted in gold. The small close button empties only that slot. Other bags keep their positions in the list, and empty slots can be filled in any order.
5. Click the wardrobe **Save** button to update the active saved look, including your latest tuner changes. For a new look, Save opens **Saved Looks** for naming.

Changing a model or color keeps its saved fit and current tuning changes.
Selecting or editing a slot keeps the Bags page open alongside the tuner.
Changing the starting position resets only that bag's fit to the new mount.
Fits are separate for each race and gender. Unsaved tuner edits are temporary;
switching looks warns before discarding them. Removing a bag discards its edits.

Existing cloth pouches retain their color and placement. The original Olive
Cloth Pouch uses the Low model. Burgundy Rucksack, Milloo Dark Leather, Milloo
Tan Leather and Milloo Scratch remain removed. Smooth Dark and Smooth Tan
models remain available. Stored looks are preserved until you save over them.
Older looks containing more than five bags load their first five available bags,
retaining those bags' placements. Saving over that look stores the five-bag limit.

The minimap right-click menu offers **Toggle Addon** and **Refresh Addon**.
Refresh Addon reloads the interface, retaining current appearance and bag-fit
changes in your unsaved look without overwriting a named saved look.
An unfinished invalid tuner value must be corrected before refreshing.

Existing single-bag looks and their old saved fits are imported automatically.
Toggle Addon hides bags while retaining the arrangement; Reset All removes them.
Bags are cosmetic attachments and do not change inventory slots, equipment
icons, tooltips, capacity or stats.

Version 3.9.2 uses renderer 30902 (DLL 3.9.2). Install the matching addon and DLL,
then fully restart the game. The /reload command cannot replace a loaded DLL.
